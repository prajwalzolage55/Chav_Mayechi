Store Policies

Transparency and trust are the foundation of our heritage kitchen. Everything you need to know about ordering from us.

Shipping & Delivery

Delivery Methods: We offer both local delivery for nearby customers and reliable courier services (via delivery apps) for customers further away. Since our authentic products are shelf-stable and packaged securely, we can safely ship far beyond our immediate area.

Delivery Charges: Delivery is not included in the product price. Customers pay the actual courier or local delivery cost separately. We will provide you with the exact shipping cost via WhatsApp when confirming your order.

Preparation & Dispatch Time: Because our items are crafted fresh, preparation and dispatch times vary depending on your order size and delivery location. We will give you a clear timeline when you finalize your order.

Privacy Policy

Your privacy is as sacred as our family recipes. Information collected during checkout (Name, Phone, Address) is used strictly for order fulfillment, delivery coordination, and providing you with the best possible service.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third-party marketers. Your authentication data is securely encrypted and managed via Google Firebase.

If you have subscribed to our newsletter, you may occasionally receive updates about seasonal specialties. You can opt out of these communications at any time.

Terms of Service

Order Confirmation: By placing an order with Chav Mayechi through our website, you agree to complete the payment via WhatsApp communication as instructed by our team.

Cancellations: Because our items are made-to-order and highly perishable, orders cannot be canceled once preparation has begun in our kitchen.

Returns & Refunds: Due to health and safety regulations surrounding food products, we cannot accept returns. However, if there is a legitimate issue with the quality or condition of your delivery, please contact us within 12 hours of receipt with a photograph, and we will make it right.
