The Story of
Chav Mayechi

Chav Mayechi started with a simple dream of bringing authentic homemade Maharashtrian taste to every home.

Through the support and inspiration of the Umed Mahila Bachat Gat initiative, we got the confidence to transform traditional homemade cooking into a growing business journey. What began in our kitchen with family recipes and passion for homemade food slowly became a mission to preserve the real taste of Maharashtra.

Preserving the
Authentic Taste

We noticed that people deeply miss the pure flavours of food made by mothers and grandmothers, especially traditional laddus and papads prepared with love and care.

With this vision, we started preparing products like Methi Laddu, Nachani Laddu, Dryfruit Laddu, Khajur Laddu, and handmade papads using quality ingredients, hygienic methods, and authentic recipes.

Empowered by
Tradition

Being connected with the Umed Bachat Gat movement encouraged us to become self-reliant and create opportunities through homemade food products.

Although Chav Mayechi is a personal business, the spirit of entrepreneurship was born from the Umed initiative.

Today, Chav Mayechi proudly represents women empowerment, traditional food culture, and the emotional taste of home-cooked food.

Delivering trust, purity, and
“Aai’s taste” in every bite.

With the love and support of our customers, we continue growing while staying connected to our roots and traditions. ✨
